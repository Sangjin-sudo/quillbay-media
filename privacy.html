Privacy Policy

App: Quillbay Labs publishing tool. Effective: 17 October 2026.

This app is used only by Quillbay Labs to publish its own posts to its own Instagram professional accounts through Meta's official Instagram API.

What data the app uses

  • Access tokens and account IDs for Quillbay Labs' own Instagram accounts, used only to publish content and to read publishing limits.
  • No data about other Instagram users is collected, stored, sold or shared. The app does not read followers, messages or comments.

Storage and retention

Tokens are stored privately on the operator's machine, never in this public repository. They are deleted when the app is no longer used or when access is removed in Instagram settings.

Data deletion

Because the app stores no third-party user data, there is nothing to delete for other users. To request deletion of anything related to your account, or with any question, contact the address below.
